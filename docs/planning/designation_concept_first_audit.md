# Concept-First / Conceptualist Evidence Audit

**Status:** INVESTIGATION COMPLETE

**Scope:** Phase 7.4.8 — Re-evaluate Concept-First / Conceptualist as richer evidence becomes available

## 1. Question

Has Phase 7 evidence introduced any legitimate signal capable of distinguishing concept-driven user appreciation—the idea, premise, or conceptual mechanism as a primary source of appeal—from existing evidence about a work's originality, depth, craft, engagement, or other scored properties?

This investigation evaluates whether the richer evidence changes the candidate's previously identified direct-evidence limitation. It does not authorize implementation, weighting changes, or new Designations.

## 2. Existing Candidate Semantics

Concept-First / Conceptualist describes users for whom the central idea, premise, conceptual mechanism, or underlying concept of a work is itself a major source of appeal.

The defining distinction is not that a work is unusual, deep, original, or complex. It is that the **idea itself** is important to why the work is valued.

This differs from:

* Boundary Explorer — unconventional or boundary-pushing territory
* Deep Diver — depth, layers, and psychological or interpretive richness
* Engagement Architect — construction in service of engagement

The candidate was previously retained because existing Originality and Depth evidence could not establish that the concept itself was a primary source of the user's appreciation.

No existing scoring dimension measures the **source of user appreciation** required by the candidate.

## 4. Concept-Driven Finding

The existing Concept-Driven Finding combines Originality and Depth.

It identifies archives whose entries demonstrate strong scores on both dimensions and describes a pattern of unusual ideas and conceptual depth.

However, it does not establish that the concept itself is the primary reason the user values those works.

It therefore remains a legitimate descriptive Finding about work characteristics, but it does not provide independent evidence for Concept-First / Conceptualist classification.

Recombining Originality and Depth into a new `concept_strength` signal would reproduce the proxy explicitly rejected during the earlier candidate evaluation.

## 5. Phase 7 Evidence Impact

Phase 7 expanded descriptive and cross-dimensional evidence through:

* score variance;
* completion distributions;
* temporal activity;
* media-type scoring relationships;
* game-specific scoring divergence;
* genre relationships;
* Designation signal audits;
* Identity signal review.

None of these evidence streams measures why the user values a work.

Phase 7.3's game-scoring investigation separates systems/mechanical qualities from dimensions such as depth and emotional impact, but this addresses systems-oriented evidence rather than concept-driven appreciation.

The direct evidence gap therefore remains unchanged.

## 6. Cross-Media Evidence

Books, games, and videos continue to provide Originality and Depth evidence, but none contains a scoring dimension whose defined semantics establish concept-as-source-of-appeal.

The candidate remains conceptually general-purpose, but the current evidence model does not provide general-purpose evidence for it.

## 7. Existing Conceptual Overlap

Available evidence is already partially represented by existing concepts:

* **Deep Diver** — depth and interpretive richness
* **Boundary Explorer** — originality and unconventional territory
* **Concept-Driven Finding** — Originality + Depth pattern
* **Interpretive Philosophy** — depth, reflection, ambiguity, and analysis
* **Boundary Preference** — originality and unusual-genre exposure

These concepts do not mean the same thing as Concept-First. Rather, they demonstrate that the currently available evidence is already represented elsewhere without establishing the candidate's defining source-of-appreciation semantics.

## 8. Remaining Evidence Gap

The archive still lacks:

1. A legitimate signal measuring that the concept, premise, or idea itself is a major reason the work is valued.
2. Evidence distinguishing concept-driven appreciation from appreciation driven by originality, depth, craft, engagement, or other qualities.
3. Negative-space cases demonstrating that high Originality and Depth do not automatically imply Concept-First appreciation.

No Phase 7 artifact currently supplies this evidence.

## 9. Disposition

**Direct evidence gap remains substantially unchanged.**

Phase 7 added richer descriptive and cross-dimensional evidence, but none of it measures the source of user appreciation required by the Concept-First definition.

The candidate remains an active Designation candidate whose direct evidence gap is still the controlling limitation.

## 10. What This Investigation Does Not Justify

This investigation does not justify:

* introducing Concept-First / Conceptualist as a Designation;
* creating a `concept_strength` proxy from Originality and Depth;
* changing existing Designation semantics;
* changing scoring weights;
* treating the Concept-Driven Finding as direct evidence of Concept-First appreciation.

## 11. Governing Rule

> **Evidence before labels. Concepts before implementation. Evolution, not rewrite.**
