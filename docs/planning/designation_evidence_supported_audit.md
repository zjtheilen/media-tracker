# Designation Evidence-Supported Pattern Audit

**Status:** INVESTIGATION COMPLETE

**Scope:** Phase 7.4.5 — Identify recurring evidence-supported patterns not represented by current Designation vocabulary

**Principle:** Evidence before labels. Concepts before implementation. Evolution, not rewrite.

## 1. Question

Does the expanded archive evidence reveal any recurring, meaningful pattern that the current Designation vocabulary does not adequately represent?

This investigation evaluates whether the evidence accumulated through Phase 7 provides sufficient conceptual justification for an additional Designation.

It does not authorize implementation changes, weighting changes, new Designations, or Identity changes.

## 2. Current Designation Vocabulary

The current Designation vocabulary consists of:

* Boundary Explorer
* Curator
* Engagement Architect
* Deep Diver

These Designations describe distinct evidence territories:

* Boundary Explorer — unusual, experimental, speculative, or boundary-oriented exploration
* Curator — archive breadth, quality, and composition
* Engagement Architect — engagement, construction, systems, and momentum-oriented evidence
* Deep Diver — depth, emotional impact, and interpretive/psychological richness

Overlap between Designations is expected. The question is not whether every observed pattern belongs exclusively to one Designation, but whether a meaningful recurring pattern exists that cannot be adequately represented by the current vocabulary.

## 3. Expanded Evidence Considered

The investigation considered the expanded evidence available across the archive, including:

* score distributions and variance
* completion-state evidence
* temporal consumption evidence
* media-type scoring relationships
* genre prevalence and affinity
* genre combinations
* universal and media-specific scoring dimensions
* normalized trait strengths
* derived Identity traits
* existing Observations and Findings
* current Designation signals and evaluator behavior

The investigation also considered evidence that is calculated but currently descriptive, under-used, or intentionally deferred.

## 4. Evidence Boundary

Calculated evidence does not automatically constitute an intelligence concept.

The investigation distinguishes between:

1. evidence that exists;
2. evidence that is calculated;
3. evidence that is consumed;
4. evidence with established semantics;
5. evidence supporting a recurring meaningful pattern;
6. evidence sufficient to justify a new Designation.

In particular, archive composition, genre exposure, scoring dimensions, completion state, temporal activity, and statistical variance cannot automatically establish intentionality, motivation, preference, personality, or behavioral philosophy.

## 5. Recurring Pattern Review

### 5.1 Systems and Construction-Oriented Evidence

The Phase 7.3 media-type × scoring investigation identified games in which relatively strong gameplay/mechanics scores coexist with lower universal depth or emotional-impact scores.

This provides evidence that gameplay/mechanical evaluation can diverge from broader scoring dimensions.

The existing `system_design` derived trait was examined as a possible representation of this pattern. It is currently only an alias of the `gameplay_mechanics` average and has no independent production consumer or established semantics representing a broader behavioral tendency toward systems or construction.

The existing Engagement Architect vocabulary provides an existing conceptual home for construction, systems, engagement, and momentum-oriented evidence.

**Finding:** The evidence does not currently establish a distinct recurring behavioral concept requiring a new Designation.

### 5.2 Score Variance and Polarization

Score variance is available as archive evidence and may reveal differences in how consistently media is evaluated.

However, variance alone does not establish a meaningful behavioral interpretation such as polarization, selectivity, experimentation, or changing preference.

No sufficiently established semantic pattern was identified that requires Designation-level representation.

**Finding:** Score variance remains descriptive evidence at the current stage.

### 5.3 Genre Combinations

`genreCombinations` provides pairwise genre co-occurrence frequencies.

This is legitimate archive evidence, but it is currently descriptive. No established interpretation rule identifies particular combinations as evidence of a distinct recurring behavioral pattern.

Existing genre-based Observations, Findings, and Designation signals already capture several related dimensions through genre prevalence, affinity, and broader trait evidence.

**Finding:** Genre combinations do not currently establish a Designation vocabulary gap.

### 5.4 Completion Evidence

Completion-state distributions provide meaningful information about archive state and consumption behavior.

However, completion status alone does not establish a stable preference, motivation, or behavioral identity. Existing Phase 7 investigations have therefore treated completion evidence descriptively rather than as automatic intelligence.

**Finding:** No additional Designation concept is justified by completion evidence.

### 5.5 Temporal Evidence

Monthly activity, media distribution, and average scores provide temporal descriptions of archive activity.

Current evidence does not establish that temporal changes represent stable changes in taste, preference, motivation, engagement, or identity.

**Finding:** Temporal evidence does not currently justify an additional Designation.

## 7. Orphaned and Under-Used Evidence

Two signals received focused follow-up review.

### `system_design`

`system_design` is currently a derived Identity trait whose value is equivalent to the archive's `gameplay_mechanics` average.

It has no current production consumer and no established independent semantic meaning.

The Phase 7.3 game-scoring evidence makes the underlying gameplay-mechanics signal more interesting, but does not by itself establish `system_design` as a distinct behavioral construct.

Its natural future evaluation point is Identity signal independence, not Designation expansion.

### `genreCombinations`

`genreCombinations` is calculated from entry-level genre co-occurrence and stored on the archive profile.

It is currently not interpreted by Designations, Identities, Observations, or Findings.

It may warrant a future focused Observation/Finding investigation if the archive becomes dense enough to reveal stable, explainable combinations that are not reducible to existing genre prevalence, affinity, or trait evidence.

It does not currently justify a new Designation.

## 8. Vocabulary Assessment

The expanded evidence does not reveal a sufficiently established recurring pattern that is absent from the current Designation vocabulary.

This is not a claim that the four Designations exhaust every observable property of the archive.

Rather, the evidence currently available does not cross the threshold required to justify adding another Designation.

Known limitations and under-used signals are evidence-boundary issues, deferred investigations, or potential future refinements rather than demonstrated vocabulary gaps.

## 9. Result

**No additional Designation concept is currently justified by the expanded evidence.**

The existing Designation vocabulary remains conceptually usable at the current evidence stage.

Several evidence streams remain available for future investigation, particularly:

* Identity signal independence involving systems/gameplay evidence;
* potential interpretation of genre combinations;
* future evidence-supported interpretation of score variance or temporal relationships.

None currently provides sufficient evidence for a new Designation.

## 10. What This Investigation Does Not Justify

This investigation does not justify:

* adding a new Designation;
* changing existing Designation weights;
* rewriting Designation evaluators;
* converting descriptive statistics into behavioral labels;
* treating genre exposure as intentional exploration;
* treating archive size or diversity as deliberate curation;
* treating gameplay-mechanics scores as evidence of a broader systems-oriented identity;
* promoting `system_design` into active Identity scoring;
* interpreting genre combinations without established semantic rules;
* changing existing Identity semantics.

## 11. Governing Rule

> **A new Designation requires a recurring, meaningful, explainable pattern that existing vocabulary cannot adequately represent.**

Calculated evidence alone is insufficient.

A signal becomes a candidate for new intelligence only when its semantics are established, its recurrence is supported, its interpretation is defensible, and its conceptual territory is not already adequately represented.

## 12. Next Investigation

The next relevant Phase 7.4 investigation is:

> **Re-evaluate Identity signal independence as richer evidence becomes available.**

The `system_design` signal may be relevant to that investigation, but no implementation or semantic change is implied by this investigation.
