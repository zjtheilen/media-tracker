# Systems / Construction Appreciation Evidence Audit

**Status:** INVESTIGATION COMPLETE

**Scope:** Phase 7.4.7 — Re-evaluate Systems / Construction Appreciation as richer evidence becomes available

## 1. Question

Does the expanded Phase 7.3 archive evidence now provide sufficiently meaningful systems/construction-oriented appreciation across multiple media types to materially narrow or close the cross-media evidence gap that previously kept Systems / Construction Appreciation as an active Designation candidate?

This investigation evaluates whether the richer evidence changes the candidate's previously identified cross-media limitation. It does not authorize implementation, weighting changes, or new Designations.

## 2. Existing Candidate Semantics

Systems / Construction Appreciation describes appreciation for the mechanisms, structures, systems, construction, progression, or underlying architecture of a work.

The defining distinction is not whether the work is engaging, but whether the **construction itself** is a meaningful source of appeal.

This differs from Engagement Architect:

* Engagement Architect describes effective construction in service of an engaging experience.
* Systems / Construction Appreciation describes appreciation for systems, mechanisms, structure, or construction themselves.

The candidate was previously retained because its strongest evidence was game-oriented and the archive did not establish that the same appreciation pattern operated consistently across books, videos, and games.

## 3. Cross-Media Evidence

| Media Type | Relevant Evidence                                                                                                                                                 | Semantic Strength                     | Limitation                                                                                                                    |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Game       | `gameplay_mechanics`, `level_design_progression`, `replayability_systems`; Phase 7.3 demonstrates divergence from universal depth and emotional-impact dimensions | Strong                                | Evidence is media-specific and does not generalize across the archive                                                         |
| Book       | `craft`, `narrative_pacing`, `world_building`                                                                                                                     | None for the candidate's core meaning | These dimensions describe craftsmanship, pacing, or world construction rather than systems/construction as a source of appeal |
| Video      | `craft`, `presentation`, cinematography, directing/editing, sound                                                                                                 | None for the candidate's core meaning | These dimensions describe execution and presentation rather than systems/construction appreciation                            |

Universal dimensions such as Craft, Engagement, Presentation, Depth, and Originality do not provide legitimate cross-media substitutes for the candidate's specific semantics.

Treating them as systems/construction proxies would introduce an interpretation not established by the scoring model.

## 4. Phase 7 Evidence Impact

Phase 7.3 established that game-specific mechanical and systems dimensions can score highly while universal depth and emotional-impact dimensions remain comparatively low.

This strengthens the observability of systems-oriented qualities **within games**.

It does not establish that the same appreciation pattern exists across books and videos.

The direct systems/construction signals remain:

* `gameplay_mechanics`
* `level_design_progression`
* `replayability_systems`

The existing `system_design` signal does not provide additional evidence because it has already been established as a direct alias of `gameplay_mechanics`.

## 5. Existing Conceptual Overlap

Available game-side evidence is already partially represented by existing intelligence concepts:

* **Engagement Architect** — engagement, craft, gameplay, and pacing
* **Systems Affinity** — strong `gameplay_mechanics`
* **Craft Appreciation** — strong craft
* **Engagement Priority** — strong engagement

These concepts do not establish Systems / Construction Appreciation as equivalent to them. Rather, they demonstrate that much of the currently observable game-side evidence already has existing conceptual representation.

## 6. Remaining Evidence Gap

A general-purpose Systems / Construction Designation would still require:

1. Cross-media signals whose defined semantics actually measure systems, mechanisms, structure, or construction.
2. Demonstrable recurrence of that appreciation outside games.
3. Evidence that meaningfully separates systems/construction appreciation from Engagement Architect.
4. No invented proxy formed by relabeling or recombining unrelated universal dimensions.

The current archive does not provide these.

## 7. Disposition

**Gap remains substantially unchanged.**

Phase 7.3 strengthened the evidence that systems/construction qualities are observable and separable within games. It did not supply the missing cross-media evidence that was the candidate's controlling limitation.

Systems / Construction Appreciation therefore remains an active Designation candidate, but its cross-media evidence gap remains unresolved.

## 8. What This Investigation Does Not Justify

This investigation does not justify:

* introducing Systems / Construction Appreciation as a Designation;
* changing existing Designation weights;
* changing scoring dimensions;
* treating Craft, Engagement, Presentation, or other universal dimensions as systems/construction proxies;
* promoting `system_design` into active Identity or Designation scoring.

## 9. Governing Rule

> **Evidence before labels. Concepts before implementation. Evolution, not rewrite.**
